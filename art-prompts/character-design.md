# PawBloom Character Design Prompts

## Art Direction Keywords
Japanese 2D kawaii anime, cozy illustration, hand-drawn feel, soft colors, clean line art, expressive, collectible, high readability, polished mobile game, chibi proportions

## Base Character Prompt Template

```
[BREED] in Japanese kawaii anime style, cute cozy game character, chibi proportions 
with large head (45-55% of body), big expressive anime eyes with shine highlights, 
small body, soft pastel colors, clean line art 2-3px weight, transparent background, 
simple shading, [POSE] pose, [EXPRESSION] expression, facing 3/4 view, 
high resolution 1024x1024, game-ready sprite
```

## Animation States

1. **Idle** — Subtle breathing, occasional blink, relaxed stance
2. **Walk** — Bouncy walk cycle, tail movement, ears following motion
3. **Happy** — Tail wagging/swishing, sparkle eyes, jumping slightly
4. **Surprised** — Wide eyes, perked ears, slight jump back
5. **Sleepy** — Droopy eyes, yawning, curled up or sitting

## Silhouette Requirements
- Pure black (#2D2D2D) fill
- Recognizable breed shape even without details
- Ears, tail, and body proportions must identify breed
- No internal details, only outline shape

## Export Specifications
- Master: 1024×1024 PNG, transparent background
- Runtime: 512×512 or 256×256 PNG
- Consistent anchor point at bottom center
- All poses same dimensions for easy swapping
